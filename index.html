How To Play

🎯 Goal: Train your ear to distinguish English "Minimal Pairs" — two words that differ by only one tricky sound!

🎮 Quick Controls:

  • Spacebar: Replay target word audio.
  • Left Arrow [←] or [A]: Launch at the Left Monster.
  • Right Arrow [→] or [D]: Launch at the Right Monster.
  • Click or Tap: Directly on the Monster cards.

🔥 Combos & Levels: Advance through 7 levels of tricky vowel and consonant contrasts. If you miss, you can review and listen to both sounds side-by-side!
